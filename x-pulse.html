    One cultural difference among the AI researchers in China is a lack of "messiah complex" compare to their SV equivalents. This difference is often mentioned as evidence that China doesn't care about AI safety, since you would meet no philosopher kings in Chinese labs, let alone see any of them rage quit publicly on social media, warn the world about human extinction, while racking up 100m+ views. That is the wrong conclusion. The researchers I've met all have a common sense view of right and wrong, safe and unsafe and want to steer the AI they are building to not to terrible things. But they don't see that as their job or preoccupation. That's their government's job. When their government issues guidelines and restrictions, they obey, they don't lobby. This is distinctly different from how safety and policy is shaped in the US, driven almost entirely by the frontier labs, OAI/Ant in particular, either via their official policy team or influential individual voices publicly writing and sharing that they think (or both).
